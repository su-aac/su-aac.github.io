## Update: The Survey Is Now Closed

Thank you so much to every parent and caregiver who took the time to complete the survey, and to everyone who helped share the invitation with families. The survey is now closed and is no longer accepting responses.

This study is part of my dissertation, and I am now analyzing the data. Each response tells a real story about what shared reading looks like at home, what helps, and what gets in the way. I'm truly grateful for the honesty and care families put into their answers.

I'm excited to share what we learn with everyone once the analysis is complete. Please stay tuned for a future post on the results!

---

## About the Study

I'm Hsiao-Ting, a doctoral student in Speech-Language Pathology at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. My dissertation focuses on home shared reading resources and experiences for families in Taiwan of children ages 3–7 who cannot rely on speech to be heard and understood.

### Who Participated?

Parents or primary caregivers of children ages 3–7 who cannot rely on speech to be heard and understood. This included children who typically say fewer than 100 words independently, as well as children who do not use spoken words. Families were welcome whether or not they were currently reading with their child.

### How?

An anonymous online survey that took about 15–20 minutes to complete.

### Purpose

To better understand parents' shared reading experiences so that future support services can better meet families' needs.

### Incentive Drawing

All participants who completed the survey were entered into a drawing. Ten winners will be randomly selected to receive a 7-Eleven e-gift card worth NT$300 each.
